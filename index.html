      How to play

      A secret plant has been chosen from among the plants observed growing wild in California. That includes flowering plants, conifers, ferns, mosses, liverworts, hornworts, and green and red algae: the whole of Archaeplastida that's found here.

      Guess any species. You'll see the smallest clade (branch of the tree of life) containing both your guess and the secret, along with how many California species that clade contains. Narrow down until you guess the exact species.

      Many clades have no formal name. They show up as unnamed clade along with the nearest named group that contains them. The tree resolves splits wherever published phylogenies support them, not just at named ranks.

      Badges: endemic native introduced

      Data

      • Species: every plant species with research-grade iNaturalist observations in California. Native/endemic/introduced status, common names, and photos are from iNaturalist.
      • Phylogeny: the Open Tree of Life synthetic tree (v16.1), which merges hundreds of published phylogenies. Where Open Tree leaves a polytomy, it's refined with compatible clades from the GBOTB seed plant megatree (Smith & Brown 2018; Jin & Qian 2022), then with iNaturalist taxonomy. Species missing from Open Tree are placed with their genus (or tribe or family).
